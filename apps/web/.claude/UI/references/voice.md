# Voice
Short, specific, in the shop's own words. Sentence case. Errors say what happened and what to do; they do not apologise.

Vocabulary: Collections (debit), Expenses (credit), Down payment, Full payment, Balance, Bale (cash advance), Roll, Yards, Size run, Job ticket, Released. Payment methods: GCash, BDO, Cheque, Cash.

| Situation | Write | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Expense without category | Choose a category. Expenses without one are left out of the monthly totals. | Oops! Something went wrong. |
| Not enough fabric | Roll R-014 has 26.00 yd. This order needs 47.25 yd. Add a roll or split the order. | Insufficient inventory. |
| Empty list | No orders this month yet. Record the first one. | Nothing to see here 🎉 |
| Payment saved | Payment recorded. Balance ₱12,500.00. | Success! |

A button and its confirmation use the same verb: "Record payment" → "Payment recorded".
